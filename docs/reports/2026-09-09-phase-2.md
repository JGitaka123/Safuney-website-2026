# Phase 2 — Catalogue

**Date:** 2026-09-09 · **Branch:** `claude/funny-thompson-2lukly` → PR to `main`

## What shipped
- Category pages `/products/[category]` with facets (zone, brand, application, pack size, availability,
  price), sort, URL-synced state, server-rendered links (work without JavaScript), pagination, a
  bottom-sheet filter panel on mobile, and honest empty states.
- Product pages `/products/[category]/[product]`: pack-size selector with live price and VAT line, unit
  price per litre/kg, stock status (in stock / low / made to order / out), quantity, Add to cart,
  dilution chart and calculator (litres of solution → product to measure), how to use, hazard notice
  with GHS-style pictogram, SDS/COA download list (or "ask us" when none is uploaded), FAQ, reviews
  (verified purchases only), frequently bought together, compatible dispensers, alternatives,
  breadcrumbs, and JSON-LD (`Product` + `Offer`, `AggregateRating` when reviews exist, `BreadcrumbList`,
  `FAQPage`). Statically generated for every visible product, revalidated every 5 minutes.
- Search: instant results as you type (debounced, keyboard-navigable combobox), typo tolerance via
  `pg_trgm`, products + categories + documents, empty-state suggestions; `/search` results page.
- Order sheet `/order-sheet`: every SKU on one page with quantity inputs and "Add N lines to cart".
- Server-side cart (cookie token, 30-day expiry): add, set quantity, remove, add many, merge on login;
  totals recomputed from the catalogue on every read; stock checked on add; withdrawn products are
  flagged in the cart rather than silently dropped. `/cart` page and header count.
- Pricing display mode (site setting, ex-VAT until the PO answers question 7), `From` prices on cards.
- Demo seed mode (`SEED_DEMO=1`) so previews and CI have reviewed products; refuses to run in production.

## Verified (local production build)
- Unit: filters, pricing, dilution, JSON-LD escaping. Integration: cart service (stock, unreviewed
  products, quantities, merge, withdrawn lines), search ranking and typo tolerance.
- Playwright + axe at 360 px then desktop: category filters and sheet, product page price switching,
  calculator, JSON-LD, add → cart → quantity → remove, stock cap, 404 for unreviewed slugs, search,
  order sheet.
- Lighthouse mobile on `/`, `/products`, a category, a product, `/cart`, `/contact`: numbers in the PR checks.

## Not done / blocked
- Product photography: tiles show the product name on a flat square until the PO's shoot (photography.md).
- Real prices, pack sizes, brands, SDS/COA files: PO catalogue import (Phase 6 admin CSV import).
- Semantic search (Voyage + pgvector) is Phase 8; Postgres full-text + trigram is live now.
