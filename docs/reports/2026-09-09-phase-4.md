# Phase 4 — B2B: accounts, approvals, price lists, credit, quotes

**Date:** 2026-09-09 · **Branch:** `claude/funny-thompson-2lukly` → PR to `main` · Plan: `docs/plans/phase-4-b2b.md`

## What shipped
- **Sign-in** (Auth.js v5, ADR 0008): six-digit code by SMS or email, or an emailed link; no customer
  passwords. Staff: email + password + authenticator code. Guest cart merges into the account on
  sign-in; the header shows Account or Sign in without making pages dynamic.
- **Organisation accounts**: owner creates the organisation (name, registered name, KRA PIN, approval
  threshold), adds buyers, approvers and owners by email; members see the organisation at checkout
  and orders are attached to it with the purchase-order number.
- **Approval workflow**: a buyer's order at or above the threshold is placed as *awaiting approval*
  with stock held for seven days; approvers and owners decide from `/account/approvals`; approval
  confirms (cash, invoice) or starts payment (M-Pesa, card); rejection cancels and releases stock;
  every step is an event with the actor. Unapproved orders are cancelled by the cron after the hold.
- **Price lists**: tiered customer prices (`PriceListItem.minQty`) resolved on the server at order
  placement; the client never sends prices. Admin tooling for price lists is Phase 6.
- **Credit accounts**: application from the account (registered name, KRA PIN, limit, terms, trade
  references, document links); finance decides at `/sales/credit`. Invoice payment is offered only to
  approved organisations and only within the available credit (limit minus unpaid tax invoices),
  checked inside the order transaction with the customer row locked. Tax invoices
  (`INV-YYYYMMDD-NNNN`, KRA fields, itemised VAT, due date from terms) are issued on confirmation and
  downloadable as PDF; `/account/credit` is the statement of account (limit, outstanding, available,
  overdue, invoices).
- **Saved lists and reorder**: any order becomes a list in one tap; lists add to the cart in one tap
  with per-line stock reporting; one-click reorder from order history.
- **Scheduled deliveries**: a cart becomes a standing order (weekly to eight-weekly); reminder email
  three days ahead; daily cron places the due orders; skip, pause, resume, cancel from the account.
- **Request for quote**: `/quote` for guests and customers (free text and/or the cart); sales prices
  each line against a catalogue pack at `/sales/quotes`; the customer accepts from an emailed link and
  the quote becomes an order at the quoted prices (audited on the order event). Quotes expire.
- Sales desk at `/sales` (quotes to price, credit applications) for SALES, FINANCE and ADMIN roles.

## Addendum — reconciling two Phase 4 implementations (and a broken deploy)

A second Phase 4 was pushed straight to `main` while this branch was in review (commits `520edf9`,
`cf5584c`). Both are now reconciled into one. This branch's implementation is kept, because the other
one shipped an authentication bypass:

```ts
export const DEMO_B2B_SESSIONS = { "demo-buyer": {...}, "demo-approver": {...} }
if (token in DEMO_B2B_SESSIONS) return DEMO_B2B_SESSIONS[token]!;
```

Setting the cookie `sfn_session=demo-approver` by hand made anyone a finance approver on a corporate
account with a KES 500,000 credit limit. The check ran before the database check and was not gated on
the environment, so it worked in production. Alongside it: `submitLoginAction` signed in any email
address without a password, code or link; `approveOrder` took an order id with no membership check, so
one approver could approve or cancel any organisation's orders; `/quotes/[number]` served a quote by
its sequential number with no token, exposing contact details and negotiated prices; and the guard
against charging an already-paid order had been removed. All of that is gone.

**Ported from it, because it was genuinely better:**
- **Stop supply.** An invoice more than `STOP_SUPPLY_GRACE_DAYS` (15) past its due date suspends credit
  ordering: available credit goes to zero, invoice payment is refused at checkout naming the invoice to
  settle, and M-Pesa, card and cash still work. Rebuilt on `Invoice.dueDate`/`paidAt` rather than the
  original's order-date arithmetic, so credit notes net off correctly, and checked with the customer row
  locked at placement, at approval and on a payment-method switch. Tested in `b2b.test.ts`.
- **Negotiated prices in the cart.** A signed-in B2B member now sees their price list on the cart and
  checkout pages, not just in the order total, through the same resolver the order transaction uses
  (quantity tiers, active lists only) so the two can never disagree.
- **Real quote and account links.** "Request a quote" across the site now goes to `/quote` instead of
  the contact form, carrying its context (SKU, product, category, search) into the first line of the
  request. The footer links to the credit page and the account.
- **Purchase-order field and sign-off warning** on the invoice option at checkout.

### The deploy has been failing since Phase 3
Vercel rejected every deployment with:

```
Hobby accounts are limited to daily cron jobs. This cron expression (*/10 * * * *)
would run more than once per day.
```

That schedule landed with Phase 3, so **the live site has been serving Phase 2 ever since**: no
checkout, no payments, none of Phase 4. Both crons are daily now, and the 30-minute stock hold no
longer depends on a cron at all — every availability check first releases expired holds on the packs it
is about to read (`lib/orders/reservations.ts`), so the window is real on any hosting plan. A test takes
the last packs after the window passes with no cron run.

## Addendum — adversarial review of the merged result

The merge above was reviewed against its own diff, which found real defects in code written during it.
All are fixed and covered by tests; the sequence is recorded because the shape of the mistakes is worth
keeping.

**Releasing stock on the read path made three latent races reachable.** The sweep had been a
once-a-day cron, where concurrency was theoretical. Running it on every availability check made it the
normal case:

- It read the order, released stock, then wrote the status back. A payment landing inside that window
  was overwritten: money taken, order returned to `PENDING_PAYMENT`, stock decremented twice. Each
  order is now claimed with a conditional update before anything is released, so an order that was
  paid or already swept no longer matches, and no status is ever written from a stale read.
- Two sweeps could both release the same order: the `Math.min(qty, stockReserved)` guard is a snapshot,
  while the decrement is relative, so under read-committed both saw the pre-release figure. The same
  claim makes exactly one caller the releaser.
- A made-to-order line reserves only what existed but recorded the full quantity, so expiry released
  stock the order never held, taking it from another order's reservation. `OrderItem.reservedQty` now
  records what was actually taken, is used everywhere stock moves, and is zeroed once spent.

Variant rows are locked in the same order in all four paths (placement, payment, dispatch, release) so
they cannot deadlock, and `OrderItem.variantId` is indexed because the sweep filters by it on every
cart add.

**A forwarded quote link could spend an organisation's credit.** Acceptance took the organisation from
the caller, so accepting while signed out attached the order to the organisation with no member role:
the approval threshold did not apply, and the credit line was drawn down by someone who never
authenticated. A buyer could sidestep their own approver the same way. The organisation now comes from
the accepter's membership: a member gets their real role and their threshold, anyone else gets a plain
order with no credit, and invoice acceptance asks them to sign in.

Also fixed: approval and rejection claim the decision inside their transaction, so two approvers cannot
both pass a check made outside it; credit notes reduce what is overdue as well as what is outstanding,
and headroom never exceeds the approved limit; an environment variable that is set but empty counts as
unset, after an empty `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` reached `new URL()` and broke the build.

### Known, not fixed here
- **Invoices are never marked paid.** `creditPosition` counts every unpaid tax invoice, and nothing in
  the app settles one, so outstanding credit only grows and every credit account eventually stops
  supplying itself. Finance recording a payment is Phase 6; until then settlement is a database update.
- **An order whose hold expired can still be paid without re-checking stock.** The window is honest —
  the customer is told the stock was released — but the payment path does not verify availability, so a
  late payment can be accepted for stock another customer has since taken. Pre-existing; worth closing
  when the warehouse view can flag a short order.
