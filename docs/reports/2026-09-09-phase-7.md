# Phase 7 — Content, SEO, performance, trust, Swahili

**Date:** 2026-09-09 · **Branch:** `claude/funny-thompson-2lukly` → PR to `main` ·
Plan: `docs/plans/phase-7-content-seo-performance.md` · Decisions: ADR 0011, ADR 0012, ADR 0002 (updated)

## What shipped
- **Indexing gate** (`lib/seo/indexing.ts`): indexing requires all three of production, `SITE_INDEXABLE=1`,
  and a site URL that is not a `*.vercel.app` host. A preview is never indexable whatever the flag says.
  Nine tests cover the matrix.
- **Sitemap and robots**: the sitemap lists static pages, active categories, published products and
  published content pages — and is **empty until the site is indexable**, because the sitemap of a
  blocked site is an inventory leak rather than an aid. Robots then becomes a real policy that still
  keeps crawlers out of account, admin, API, cart, checkout, orders, quotes, sales, sign-in and warehouse.
- **Canonical, hreflang and Open Graph** in the root metadata and on every new page.
- **Per-product share cards** generated from the product itself — name, cleaning zone, real pack sizes —
  in the site's own palette and typeface. There are no product photographs yet, and a link with no image
  is a link nobody clicks.
- **Solutions by industry**: hospitality, healthcare, foodservice, education, industrial and facilities
  management. Each assembled from the real catalogue (only categories that actually hold something a
  customer can buy) with `FAQPage` and `BreadcrumbList` structured data.
- **Resources**: the colour-coding guide, dilution charts generated from each product's own guidance with
  per-pack yields, and the SDS library.
- **Trust strip** on the home page: where we are, how to reach a person, delivery and returns, how you can
  pay, and the tax identifiers once the PO sets them.
- **Swahili** (`/sw`): a route group sharing the same server components, one catalogue holding both
  languages, a language switcher using real links, and `guard:i18n` in CI.
- **Revalidation**: catalogue and stock changes push straight out to the cached public pages through the
  Phase 6 admin seam.

## Two judgement calls worth your attention

**Nothing was invented.** The brief asks for compliance pages, case studies and an SDS library, and says
only PO-confirmed certifications and real approved case studies may ship. It also forbids placeholder
text. So the SDS library is a real page that says we have not published the sheets yet, because we will
not put up a sheet nobody has checked against the product in the drum — and offers to send the current
versions the same working day. `localBusinessJsonLd()` returns nothing at all today, and a test asserts
it: every company fact was harvested from search-engine snippets, which is good enough for a footer a
human reads in context and not good enough to assert to Google as fact. Flipping `poConfirmed` is the
whole change once you confirm them (ADR 0011).

**Tag-based revalidation was specified; paths were used instead.** Tagged caching means
`unstable_cache`, which serialises through JSON, and every price here is a `bigint` of minor units —
`JSON.stringify` throws on it. I verified that rather than assuming it. Tagging catalogue queries would
mean converting money to strings going in and back coming out, adding a conversion at exactly the
boundary where a rounding mistake becomes a wrong price charged to a customer. Product pages are already
ISR at 300 seconds, so `revalidatePath` from the admin seam gives the same freshness with none of that
risk (ADR 0012). If you would rather have tags, say so and I will do it — but money would have to stay
out of the cached value.

## Not done
- **Core Web Vitals field monitoring.** `@vercel/speed-insights` is already wired and reports real-user
  vitals to the Vercel dashboard; a second collection endpoint would duplicate it without adding
  anything you would look at. If you want vitals in the database instead, that is a small addition.
- **Product copy in Swahili.** Navigation, the home page, cart and checkout terms are translated; product
  descriptions stay English until you have Swahili copy, and the Swahili home page says so rather than
  pretending otherwise.
- **Image CDN.** Nothing to serve yet — uploads arrive with Vercel Blob, still blocked on you.
- **Careers and case-study pages.** Deliberately not built as empty shells: they are content pages you can
  create at `/admin/content` the day you have something real to put in them.

## Risks I see that you didn't ask about
- **The share card is generated at request time.** If the catalogue is very large and links are shared in
  bursts, each unique product's first card costs a render. Vercel caches them after that; it is only worth
  thinking about if it shows up in the logs.
- **Turning on indexing publishes the catalogue as it stands.** Anything still marked "needs review" is
  excluded from the sitemap, but a published product with a provisional price is indexed with that price.
  Correct the catalogue before you set the flag, not after.
- **`/sw` is a partial translation and search engines will index it as a page.** A Swahili speaker landing
  on it from search finds Swahili navigation and English product pages. That is honest and it is stated on
  the page, but it is worth knowing before you promote the URL.
