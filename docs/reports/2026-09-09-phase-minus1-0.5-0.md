## Risks I see that you didn't ask about
- The phone number, email and office address on the holding page came from search snippets of your site, not from the site itself. If any is stale it is now on the new site too — please confirm (question 1).
- The old site's category URLs are query strings (`products.php?a=…`) indexed under four host/scheme variants. The redirect map at launch must cover all of them or you lose the little ranking you have.
- Next 16 rewrites `tsconfig.json` on first build (sets `jsx: react-jsx`). That change is committed; if a future build reports "reconfigured your tsconfig" in CI it means someone edited it by hand.
- ESLint is pinned to 9.x because Next's ESLint config does not yet support ESLint 10 (ADR 0003).
