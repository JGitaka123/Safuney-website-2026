# Phase 5 — Customer account, orders, delivery

**Date:** 2026-09-09 · **Branch:** `claude/funny-thompson-2lukly` → PR to `main` · Plan: `docs/plans/phase-5-account-orders-delivery.md`

## What shipped
- **Address book** (`/account/addresses`): labelled delivery addresses with recipient and phone,
  default address, edit and remove; shared by organisation members. Checkout offers the saved
  addresses at the delivery step and fills the form; the order carries the recipient. Scheduled
  deliveries can now be delivered to a saved address instead of collected.
- **Warehouse** (`/warehouse`, WAREHOUSE and ADMIN roles): four lanes (paid to pick, cash or invoice
  to pick, packed, out for delivery), a pick list per order snapshotted on first view and printable
  with tick boxes, then packed → dispatched (rider name and phone) → delivered (receiver's name,
  proof-of-delivery photo link). Cash-on-delivery collection is recorded with the M-Pesa reference as
  a payment. Stock leaves at dispatch for cash and invoice orders, and at payment for M-Pesa and card,
  never twice. Every step is an event with the actor and shows on the customer's timeline.
- **Notifications**: packed, dispatched (with the rider's details and, for cash orders, the amount to
  have ready) and delivered, by email and SMS, each skipped cleanly when the provider is not
  configured; the "payment received" message from Phase 3 completes the set.
- **Profile and privacy** (`/account/profile`): name, email, phone (a changed contact is re-verified
  at the next sign-in), marketing opt-in with a timestamp; data export as JSON; account deletion that
  anonymises what tax records require (orders, invoices) and removes the rest, refused while an order
  is still in progress.
- Order history: one-click reorder and save-as-list (Phase 4) plus the fulfilment events above.

## Verified (local production build, mock payments and mock sign-in)
- Gate tests: warehouse transitions are staff-only; dispatch moves stock exactly once for cash and
  invoice orders and not at all for paid M-Pesa orders; cash collection needs the right amount and is
  recorded once; the queue and pick list are staff-only. Notification helpers degrade to "off" without
  providers. 106 unit and integration tests in `apps/web`, 33 in the packages.
- Playwright + axe, mobile first and desktop: address book (save, default, use at checkout, second
  address, remove); warehouse (customer kept out; pick → pack → dispatch → record cash → deliver, and
  the customer's timeline follows); profile (update, export, wrong confirmation refused, deletion,
  the number then opens a fresh account). 112 e2e tests green.
- Lighthouse: unchanged pages; see the PR checks.
